# The Ultimate Number
This mod adds a number scrap. Its model? The same as its value. Its value? Any number from 1-2,147,483,647 (weight configurable). It updates upon its value changing.<br/>
It also plays audio based on what number it is, picked from a selection of funny sounding numbers, often ones that have been memed to death or that my friend group in particular finds funny. At some point I'll add the option to add your own audio clips.<br/>
Special numbers (numbers bigger than 9 that have meme status, ex. 69) will often play their own unique audio clips instead of reading out each number individually. Support for adding your own "special" audio clips will be added in a future version.<br/>
